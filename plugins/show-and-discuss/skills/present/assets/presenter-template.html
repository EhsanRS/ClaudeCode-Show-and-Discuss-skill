Under the hood

How it works

No flash of the wrong theme: a 6-line inline script in <head> sets data-theme before first paint.
Here's how it fits together. [[spotlight flow]] The toggle writes the preference through a small hook, [[point store]] which saves it locally and syncs it to the user's profile. The value lands on the page as a theme attribute, and our existing colour tokens switch from there. [[spotlight no-flash]] A tiny script in the page head applies the theme before first paint. That's what prevents the white flash you saw in the demo.
